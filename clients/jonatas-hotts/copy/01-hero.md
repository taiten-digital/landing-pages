### Execution notes
- "Um personal que se adapta a você, não o contrário" carries the strategy's
  Core Promise line ("meets you where you are") without leaning on the bare
  "que funciona" claim from his Instagram bio — deliberately not reused here
  per brand-dna.md's explicit guidance not to amplify an unproven efficacy
  claim unadorned.
- Subheadline does double duty: states the general-fitness/everyday-goals
  audience plainly (strategy Messaging Hierarchy #1) and previews both
  delivery formats, so even a visitor who doesn't scroll past Hero gets the
  page's single biggest differentiator.
