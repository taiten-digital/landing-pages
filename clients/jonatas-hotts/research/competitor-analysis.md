## Conventions & Gaps Observed Across the Set
- **WhatsApp is the universal CTA in the local (Londrina) tier.** Every in-person/local competitor found (Vêneto, Talita Lopes, and by inference the broader GuiaFix set) routes to WhatsApp, matching Jonatas's own existing CTA pattern — this is not a gap to fix, it's confirmed category convention worth keeping.
- **No local Londrina competitor found publishes pricing anywhere public.** This matches what company-intelligence.md already found for Jonatas himself — pricing-via-WhatsApp is market-normal locally, not a Jonatas-specific weakness.
- **No local Londrina competitor found shows testimonials, review counts, or before/after proof on their own site/bio.** Where local competitors build trust at all, it's through *stated credentials* (degree, postgraduate study, years of experience) rather than third-party proof — this is the credible playbook available to Jonatas too, if/when he can supply real credential details (CREF number, formal education) to the client-verification step, per the existing PROOF NEEDED flag in company-intelligence.md.
- **The GuiaFix "5.0 average" is a thin signal, not a strong one.** Direct inspection shows each of the 16 listed trainers (including Jonatas and Talita Lopes) has exactly **1 review** contributing to that 5.0 — i.e., "16 competitors averaging 5.0 stars" means 16 single-review listings, not a deep, contested review category. This refines (doesn't overturn) the earlier framing in company-intelligence.md: the market is real and comparison-shopped (multiple directories/marketplaces exist for it), but GuiaFix's specific star signal shouldn't be read as meaning competitors have deep, battle-tested review histories — none of the ones checked do. **[Observation, corrects prior framing]**
- **National online-coaching brands (Treino Ninja, Consultoria Personal) operate in a completely different convention tier**: published tiered pricing, numeric review counts, before/after galleries, named supporting staff (nutritionist), guarantees, and third-party trust badges (Reclame Aqui). None of that exists anywhere in the local Londrina set. Since Jonatas offers online coaching too, this is the tier his online offer will implicitly be compared against by anyone who shops around online — even though his in-person offer competes against a much lower bar.
- **A named sub-niche (Vêneto's police-exam prep, Talita Lopes's 50+/spine focus) is a differentiator every local competitor with one uses deliberately** — nobody in this set is a pure undifferentiated generalist by choice.

## Differentiation Opportunities
What Jonatas could credibly claim that this competitor set doesn't, or doesn't clearly:
- **A real proof stack, if the client can supply it.** No local competitor publishes CREF number, formal credentials, testimonials, or a review count — even a single verified testimonial or a stated (confirmed, not assumed) CREF number would put Jonatas ahead of every local trainer checked in this pass, not just at parity. This depends entirely on what the client can verify — do not add without confirmation (see company-intelligence.md's PROOF NEEDED flags).
- **Explicitly distinguishing the in-person vs. online offer** — every local competitor that offers both (Talita Lopes, Vêneto, RP Fit, Jonatas himself) bundles them into one undifferentiated bio line rather than explaining what each actually includes. A landing page that clearly answers "what do I get if I choose online vs. in-person" is currently unclaimed territory locally.
- **A stated niche**, matching the pattern (not a gap) that Vêneto and Talita Lopes both already use. Per company-intelligence.md, Jonatas's own content already leans toward general health/conditioning/weight-loss rather than competitive/physique training — that's workable as a stated audience focus, but is currently implicit rather than declared anywhere public.
- **What NOT to chase**: matching Treino Ninja/Consultoria Personal's published-pricing-plus-review-count model would require proof (a real review count, a real client count) Jonatas doesn't currently have publicly, and would also break from the local WhatsApp-inquiry convention that every nearby competitor and Jonatas's own existing CTA already rely on. The lazier, honest move is to keep the local convention (WhatsApp CTA, no fabricated numbers) and differentiate on proof-if-available and clarity of offer, not on imitating a template-based national brand's scale signals Jonatas can't back up.
- **Faith-based identity** (already flagged in company-intelligence.md as a Jonatas-specific trait, pending client input on prominence) — not observed in any competitor's bio in this set, so if the client wants it foregrounded, it would be genuinely distinctive locally, not just personal color.
