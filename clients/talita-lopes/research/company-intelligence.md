# Company Intelligence — Talita Lopes

## Business Model
Solo-operator personal training business in Londrina/PR, Brazil. Two service
lines: (1) in-person personal training — home, condominium ("atendimento em
condomínios"), or partner studio — and (2) online consulting (remote training
plans/monitoring). Around 6 named service variants appear consistently across
both the official site and the v1 landing page: in-person personal training,
online consulting, strength/hypertrophy program prescription, weight-loss
program prescription, running training plans, and physical assessment
("avaliação física"). Free physical assessment is the funnel entry point,
booked via WhatsApp — no e-commerce, no online checkout found. **[Fact —
official site + v1 landing page]**

Revenue is presumably subscription/session-based (typical for this category
in Brazil — monthly plans for in-person, one-time or monthly for online
consulting) but no pricing, packages, or billing cadence is published on any
channel found (official site, v1 landing page, Linktree, Instagram bio,
Facebook). **[Unknown — confirms discovery questionnaire's open question;
appears to be a deliberate choice, common in this category, price disclosed
only via WhatsApp conversation]**

## Market Position
Category: independent/boutique personal trainer (not a gym or franchise),
positioned on individualized, adaptive programming rather than generic
group training. Stated tagline on v1 page: "Your training needs to adapt to
you. Not the other way around" ("O seu treino precisa se adaptar a você. Não
o contrário" — paraphrased from English-summarized extraction). Official
site headline: "Resultados que fazem a diferença, com atendimento
especializado" / "Cuide do seu corpo com quem entende de saúde!" **[Fact —
both live sites]**

Core differentiation claim: specialist in exercise for spinal-column
disorders ("Especialista em exercício físico para distúrbios da coluna
vertebral"), plus explicit focus on the 50+ segment and gerontology
specialization. This is a fairly distinctive niche for the local market —
most competitor listings found via search (see Sources) advertise generic
hypertrophy/weight-loss/functional training without a spine- or
older-adult-specific angle. **[Fact for the claim itself; Inference for it
being a market differentiator, since a full competitor set wasn't
benchmarked in this pass]**

  without success. **[Inference, supported by testimonial language quoted
  below]**

  (dedicated "planilha de treino para corredores" service line). **[Fact —

    comecei a treinar com orientação." — sciatica/back-pain relief as a
    concrete outcome.
  **[Fact that these appear on the v1 landing page, attributed there to

  age-related hesitation about starting or resuming exercise. **[Inference]**
