# Discovery Questionnaire — {{CLIENT_NAME}}

> Generated per-project by the `create-client` workflow. Research first — only
> ask what can't reliably be discovered externally. Prioritize by importance,
> and adapt question types to the business (a SaaS ≠ a restaurant ≠ a clinic).

## Business Overview
- What does {{CLIENT_NAME}} sell, and to whom?
- What is the single primary goal of this landing page?

## Goals
- What action should a visitor take? (buy, book, call, sign up, request quote…)
- What does success look like in 90 days?

## Audience
- Who is the primary buyer? What do they already know/believe?
- Where will traffic come from (ads, organic, referral, social)?

## Offer
- What exactly is being offered, and at what price/terms?
- What is the sales process after the CTA is clicked?

## Proof & Differentiators
- What makes {{CLIENT_NAME}} different from alternatives?
- What proof exists (testimonials, cases, certifications, guarantees)?

## Objections & Constraints
- What objections do prospects raise most often?
- Any compliance, legal, or brand constraints on claims/imagery?

## Assets Available
- Logo, photography, existing copy, brand guidelines — what already exists?

## Open Questions
- (Filled in during discovery — anything research could not resolve.)
