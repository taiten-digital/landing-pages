Purpose (page-architecture.md): let voice + (if sourced) real demonstration
footage carry the trust-building this page can't do with testimonials or

**Section heading**
Como é treinar com o Jonatas

**Body**
Sem papo de "academia intimidadora". O Jonatas corrige a sua postura, ajusta
a pegada, explica por que aquele exercício está doendo no lugar errado —
tudo na hora, sem enrolação.

Se você é sedentário há um tempo, enjoou da rotina ou nem sabe por onde
começar, o treino é montado a partir disso: ajustado ao que você consegue
fazer hoje, não ao que um atleta faria.

Nos vídeos dele, dá pra ver esse jeito de ensinar na prática — correção de
postura, dica de execução, direto ao ponto.

### Execution notes
- "Enjoei do exercício, e agora?" (brand-dna.md's documented caption hook)
  is echoed in spirit ("enjoou da rotina") rather than quoted verbatim as a
  testimonial — it's his own established content theme, not a customer
  quote, so it's safe to paraphrase but should not be dressed up as a review.
- The "nos vídeos dele" line references his actual, verified public content
  style (candid demonstration/technique-correction clips per brand-dna.md) —
  true as a description of his existing content, not a claim about results.
- Deliberately does NOT include any certification, credential, or "trained/
  qualified professional" language. PROOF NEEDED: CREF number. Strategy
  (Proof to Use #4, Messaging Hierarchy #4) is explicit that if the client
  confirms a CREF number before launch, it becomes a single line here (or in
  "How it starts") — e.g. "CREF nº _____ /PR." — not a new section. Until
  confirmed, do not state or imply certification, per brand-dna.md's Don't
  list and this project's content-integrity rule.
- Imagery: page-architecture.md flags that if visual-research can't source
  real Jonatas photo/video, this section needs to lean more on copy than
  imagery. The body copy above is written to stand on its own without
  requiring a photo/video to make its point — if real footage becomes
  available, treat it as reinforcement, not a dependency.
- This section intentionally does not answer "is he legit" with hard proof —
  per strategy.md, that objection currently can't be fully answered, and
  papering over it with vague credential-adjacent language would misrepresent
  what's actually verified. Tone and demonstrated teaching style are the
  honest answer available right now.
