# Competitor Analysis — Talita Lopes

## Scope & Method Note
Searched the Londrina/PR personal-trainer market via: the directory named in
the brief (acheioprofissional.com.br/personal-trainer/londrina), general
marketplaces (Superprof, Cronoshare, StarOfService), and targeted searches
for "personal trainer Londrina" + spine/coluna/idosos/50+ keywords, followed
by direct Instagram bio fetches to confirm each name is a real, locatable
professional (not a fabricated or generic listing). All fetches below are
dated 2026-09-15. Instagram/marketplace pages were retrieved through a
webpage-summarization tool rather than raw HTML, so exact bio wording should
be treated as paraphrase-accurate, not always verbatim — flagged where it
matters. **[Methodology]**

## Per-Competitor Notes

### Marcelo Alexandre do Nascimento (closest positioning overlap found)
- Hero message / CTA: Pain-elimination promise (coluna, lombar, ombro, joelho) via Superprof marketplace profile; contact routed through the marketplace, not a dedicated site.
- Proof shown: UEL degree; in-progress postgraduate certification. No testimonials surfaced.
- Objections addressed: Implicitly targets people currently in pain from spine/joint issues, similar to Talita's target complaint.
- Visual language: Unknown — marketplace-template profile, not a custom site (fetch blocked/403 on direct page load).
- Information architecture: Marketplace listing format (bio, rate, credentials, contact button) — not comparable to a custom landing page.
- Strengths: Specific, credible-sounding pain/rehab claim; local (Londrina).
- Weaknesses: No dedicated branded site found (Superprof-only presence limits control over presentation and SEO); no explicit 50+ or lifestage framing — the niche is pain-type, not age-cohort.

## Differentiation Opportunities
- **The specific combination Talita claims — spinal-column specialization AND explicit 50+ branding, together, on an owned website — was not found matched by any confirmed Londrina competitor.** The two closest overlaps split the niche in half: Marcelo Alexandre do Nascimento owns the pain/spine angle (but doesn't brand around age), and Fernando Azevedo owns the aging angle (but pairs it with pregnancy training, not spine, and has no dedicated site). No local competitor combines both the way Talita's messaging does. **[Inference, based on a real but not exhaustive competitor set — see Unknowns below before stating "no one else does this" as an absolute claim on the page]**
- **Owned website vs. marketplace/Instagram-only presence is itself a differentiator.** Every confirmed local PT competitor found in this pass operates through Instagram and/or a generic marketplace profile (Superprof/Cronoshare) with no independent branded site. Talita already has two (the official site and the v1 landing page) — the rebuild should lean into this as a credibility/professionalism gap versus the local competitive set, not just versus the niche claim. **[Observation]**
- Local market pricing signals (Superprof/Cronoshare aggregate data) suggest a going rate around R$76–R$120/hour for general personal training in Londrina; Talita's continued no-published-pricing approach is consistent with local norms, not an outlier. **[Observation, useful context, not a differentiation lever either way]**

## Unknowns / Gaps to Flag for the Discovery Questionnaire
- The directory/marketplace search in this pass is broad but not exhaustive — Londrina has dozens of independent trainers with no online footprint the tools could find (word-of-mouth/referral-only operators are inherently under-indexed). Recommend treating "no local competitor combines spine + 50+" as **strong but not absolute** — ask the client directly whether she knows of any local trainer who markets specifically to the 50+/spinal-health segment, since she would have first-hand market knowledge this research can't fully replicate.
- @qualividaaoidoso's location could not be confirmed — if it turns out to be Londrina-based, it would be the most direct senior-focused competitor found (though still not spine-specific). Worth a quick client check ("have you heard of this account?") rather than further automated digging.
- Ana Célida da Silva (Cronoshare) and "AM Corpo e Movimento" (acheioprofissional) both have descriptions that hint at rehab/special-population work but couldn't be independently verified via a primary page in this pass — flagged as gaps, not claims.
