Category: branding · When to use: phase 4 of `/create-client`, after
`research/company-intelligence.md` exists · Inputs: client site/social
material, `research/company-intelligence.md` · Outputs: `brand/brand-dna.md` ·
Dependencies: WebFetch, `mcp__claude-in-chrome__*` for visual capture ·
Limitations: describes what exists, does not invent brand attributes not
evidenced in source material.

## Method
1. Visual DNA: colors, typography, contrast, spacing/grid, photography style,
   iconography, composition, density — observed, not assumed.
2. Verbal DNA: tone, vocabulary, sentence structure, headline/CTA style,
   formality, repeated expressions, words the brand avoids.
3. Commercial DNA: core promise, main argument/mechanism, differentiators,
   proof style, how objections are handled, offer language.
4. Place the brand on relevant personality spectrums (formal↔casual,
   playful↔serious, premium↔accessible).
5. Write explicit Do/Don't guidance so later copy and design agents stay
   consistent without re-deriving this analysis.
6. Goal is recognizability and extension, not imitation — flag where the
   existing brand is inconsistent or underdeveloped rather than papering
   over it.
