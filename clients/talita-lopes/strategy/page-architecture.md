# Page Architecture — Talita Lopes

> Per strategy.md Section 9: short, focused page. Offer is low-friction
> (free WhatsApp assessment), trust required is moderate not high, traffic
> is warm/organic. This does not license a long cold-traffic persuasion
> sequence (no problem-agitation block, no pricing-justification, no
> competitor comparison table, no extended before/after). It also doesn't
> license padding back up to a "safe" 8 sections for its own sake — v1's
> section list is the audit's starting point, not its answer.

## 1. Minimum-Necessary-Page Audit

Candidates drawn from v1's existing sections (nothing generic added) and
evaluated against the messaging hierarchy and objections in strategy.md.

**Result: 6 sections survive** (Hero, About, Services, Testimonials, FAQ,
Contact — with "How it works" folded into the Contact/CTA section as a
compact 3-step explainer immediately before the WhatsApp button, since both
are offer-mechanics content sequenced right before the ask and neither
needs its own scroll stop on a short page). Differentiators is cut as a
standalone section; its content lives inside Hero and About instead.

This lands one section under v1's 8, not over — consistent with "re-
sequenced, not expanded," and with the fact that a section restating
already-stated differentiation failed its own audit line.

## 2. Final Section Order

1. **Hero**
2. **About Talita (credentials)**
3. **Testimonials**
4. **Services**
5. **FAQ**
6. **How it works + Contact/CTA** (combined final block)

Rationale for this order, tied to buyer sophistication and traffic
(strategy Section 1 and 9): visitor arrives warm (Instagram bio/referral),
moderate sophistication, moderate trust bar. The order front-loads the one
claim that makes this page different (Hero), immediately backs it with
proof it's real (About), then answers the primary segment's most likely
skepticism with the exact testimonial built for it (Testimonials) before
broadening to the secondary segment's services. FAQ mops up the one
objection nothing else on the page answers, positioned right before the
ask — not earlier, where it would interrupt momentum toward a claim the
visitor hasn't yet been given reason to have questions about.

## 3. Per-Section Notes

### 1. Hero
**Communicates:** the combined claim — training adapts to the person, not
the reverse (mechanism) + spinal-column exercise specialization + explicit
50+/age-inclusive framing — as one coherent position, not three separate
lines. Primary CTA button present but not the section's only job.
**What breaks if removed:** there is no page. This carries hierarchy items
#1–#3 and is the only section required to do so in the first viewport per
strategy.md.
**Visual treatment:** light (sage/white) asymmetric ~60/40 split with a
diagonal clip-path seam and bleed photo — see design-direction.md.

### 2. About Talita (credentials)
**Communicates:** CREF number, UNOPAR Physical Education degree, +10 years
in Londrina — stated plainly, positioned as *proof of* the Hero's
specialization claim, not as the hook itself. Gerontology specialization
and exact CREF string are flagged in strategy.md as unconfirmed — this
section should not originate wording for either until client sign-off.
**What breaks if removed:** the specialization claim in Hero has nothing
backing it; a moderate-trust, health-adjacent offer with an unbacked claim
is a real credibility gap, not a cosmetic one.
**Visual treatment:** sage tint with a blob-masked photo overlapped by a
dark-forest color block carrying stat-styled credential callouts — see
design-direction.md.

### 3. Testimonials
**Communicates:** 2–3 of the existing quotes, led with "passei mais de dois
anos com outros profissionais sem o resultado que queria" specifically
because it preempts objection #1. The sciatica and "ouve e adapta"
quotes follow, covering objections #2 and #3. Attribution stays as "Google
review" per strategy — do not restate the "28 reviews / 5 stars" count
unless the client reconfirms it first (flagged as possibly stale).
**What breaks if removed:** the primary segment's most probable objection
("I've tried this before and it didn't work") goes unanswered by anything
concrete; the page falls back on the trainer's own claims with no third-
party corroboration.
**Visual treatment:** dark forest background with a sticky stat rail beside
a horizontal CSS scroll-snap testimonial strip (no JS) — see
design-direction.md.

### 4. Services
**Communicates:** the 6 real service lines (in-person PT, online
consulting, hypertrophy, weight-loss, running plans, physical assessment),
kept brief per hierarchy #6 — not the differentiated hook, just confirms
breadth for the secondary segment. Online consulting should be framed
toward the secondary (non-50+-specific) segment per strategy's suggested
handling of objection #4, since this is the section where that scoping
decision is visible, not asserted as solved.
**What breaks if removed:** the secondary segment (hypertrophy/weight-loss/
runners — a real, undropped revenue line per strategy) has no evidence the
page serves them at all.
**Visual treatment:** white background, asymmetric bento grid with the
"Avaliação gratuita" cell featured 2x2 in accent-green — see
design-direction.md.

### 5. FAQ
**Communicates:** whatever remains unanswered by the narrative sections
above — chiefly objection #4 ("is online consulting as effective as
in-person for someone 50+"), which strategy.md explicitly flags as having
no source material to draw from. This section is the deliberate landing
spot for a short reassurance line once the client/copywriter supplies one;
absent that, keep online consulting scoped to the secondary segment in
Services rather than forcing an unsupported claim here. Also covers
practical logistics (is the assessment really free, any commitment).
**What breaks if removed:** the one objection in the whole strategy
document with no answer anywhere else on the page stays completely
unaddressed, with no mechanism left to even attempt a resolution.
**Visual treatment:** sage-alt tint, sticky-split layout — reassurance copy
and a plain rounded-rect photo beside the accordion — see
design-direction.md.

### 6. How it works + Contact/CTA (combined)
**Communicates:** a compact 3-step explainer of what happens after
messaging (reply → free assessment scheduled → personalized plan),
immediately followed by the single conversion action — "Marcar avaliação"
via WhatsApp, first-name framed ("Fale com a Talita"). No pricing, no
secondary CTA, no competing action.
**What breaks if removed:** without the brief process explainer, a visitor
is asked to message a stranger with no idea what follows — adding friction
to the one deliberately low-friction ask the entire page is built around.
Without the CTA itself, there is no conversion event at all.
**Visual treatment:** dark forest background, single centered accent-green
pill CTA, 3-step process as a horizontal connected-dot timeline (not equal
cards) — see design-direction.md.

## 4. What Was Deliberately Left Out

- **Standalone Differentiators section** — cut; its two real points (spine+
  50+ combo, adaptive mechanism) are Hero's content, and its third point
  (owned page vs. Instagram-only competitors) is a structural advantage the
  page demonstrates by existing, not a claim to assert in prose.
- **Pricing/packages section** — strategy Section 9 is explicit that price
  isn't disclosed and isn't the page's job to justify; no ROI-anchoring
  section is needed for an offer with no visible price.
- **Extended "problem agitation" / before-after section** — appropriate for
  cold, unsophisticated traffic that needs to be convinced the problem is
  real before it will read a solution; this traffic is warm and the primary
  segment already knows its own back/joint history. Testimonials do this
  job more credibly and in less space.
- **Competitor comparison** — the differentiator is meant to read as
  implicit rarity, not an asserted "we're better than X" claim (strategy is
  explicit the local competitor set isn't exhaustively verified); a
  comparison section would overstate a position strategy deliberately
  hedges.
